.insatiably-curious

A winding road, on purpose

I trained as a chemical engineer. At IIT Roorkee I spent my research years coaxing microalgae into producing more oil for biodiesel, and published two papers on it.

Then I got curious about how systems of people and data fit together, which led me to business analysis, to blockchain, and now to AI. The subject keeps changing. The urge to understand how things work hasn't.

My child is my favourite teacher right now. Watching someone meet the world for the first time makes you ask better questions.

Whatever titles I collect, I'll always be my dad's little princess. His belief in me is the quiet confidence I carry into every room, and the kind of belief I now try to give my own child.
