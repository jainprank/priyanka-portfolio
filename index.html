GenAI · AgenticThe Hashgraph Group2025–now

Flowgenix-AI

A multi-agent GenAI system that turns dense carbon-credit methodology documents into executable workflows.

  • Before a carbon methodology could go live on a registry, subject-matter experts had to translate a long methodology PDF into workflows, forms, eligibility rules and formulas by hand.
  • It took 4–5 months per methodology, which made onboarding the bottleneck for growth in carbon markets.
  • Split the job across specialist agents instead of one general prompt: a parser builds a structured document model, then generators produce BPMN, forms, validation rules, DMN and calculation logic.
  • Added human-in-the-loop checkpoints between phases so experts review the output instead of authoring it, keeping trust and accuracy where they matter.
  • Methodology processing dropped from 4–5 months to 1–2 days, about 98% faster.
  • The expert's role moved from writing every artifact to reviewing what the agents produce, which speeds up carbon-market onboarding on EcoGuard.

Digital identity · GenAI deliveryAccenture → THG2023–now

Tokenized identity wallet + IDTrust

A tokenized digital-identity platform for a major Japanese telecom, for issuing and verifying credentials across enterprises and governments, followed by IDTrust, a decentralized identity product at THG.

  • Identity products have to satisfy many stakeholders at once: engineering, UX, QA, the client, and the issuers and verifiers on both sides of every credential.
  • Writing stories and test cases by hand slowed every sprint, and delivery risks often surfaced late.
  • Owned scope and priorities each sprint, turning input from every stakeholder group into delivery plans and acceptance criteria.
  • Brought GenAI into the delivery process: auto-generated user stories and test cases, and dashboard analytics to flag risks and capacity gaps early. At THG I built IDTrust with Claude, from requirements through to delivery.
  • Shipped a tokenized credential platform that serves both enterprises and governments.
  • Set up a repeatable AI-first delivery model, from spec to stories to tests, that I now use across the THG portfolio.

Carbon markets · HederaThe Hashgraph Group2025–now

EcoGuard carbon registry

A carbon-credit registry on Hedera, part of the three-product enterprise suite I own alongside TrackTrace and Flowgenix-AI.

  • Carbon markets depend on trust between six very different parties: registries, methodology developers, validation and verification bodies, project proponents, buyers and platform admins.
  • Each one needs a different workflow, and every credit has to be traceable from issuance to retirement.
  • Designed the product around each stakeholder's role and job to be done, with Hedera providing an immutable audit trail.
  • Prioritized across the portfolio so Flowgenix-AI would remove EcoGuard's biggest onboarding bottleneck, and ran gap analyses against national-registry requirements to shape the backlog.
  • Took EcoGuard live with clients across India, the Philippines and Latin America, including a state-government agency.
  • Combined with Flowgenix-AI, onboarding a new methodology now takes days rather than months.

Approach

Product judgment over process.

I'm a Certified Scrum Product Owner, but I use frameworks as tools, not as goals. These principles guide how I decide.

Every item has to trace back to a user or business problem worth solving. If it can't, it doesn't make the roadmap, however loudly someone asks for it.

Eight years of delivery taught me what ideas actually cost to build. I weigh value against effort and risk before committing a team, not after.

AI should do the heavy lifting, and experts should own the judgment calls. Flowgenix-AI is built around that split, because that split is what makes the output trustworthy enough to use.

Engineering, design and executives align faster when they can see what we're not doing and why. I write the trade-off down before anyone asks.
